Why would this machine need a spacer?

The Universal CNC Axis wiki names 6 × 12 × 4 mm flanged bearings in its build list. Its 2017 parts section calls for a nominal 1 mm spacer when using 6 × 10 × 3 mm bearings.

Check the source: our retained spacer has a 1.016 mm pad and a 12.7 mm bore. That bore is larger than the named 10 mm bearing, so these records alone do not establish a contacting pair. The 2020 idler is a different revision.
